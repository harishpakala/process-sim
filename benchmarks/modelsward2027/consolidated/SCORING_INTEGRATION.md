# Offline scoring integration — stage 1
score_runs.py aggregates recorded positional comparisons using scenario contracts, retains all attempts, rejects duplicate method/model/query IDs, and produces a per-query review queue. It intentionally does not infer semantic correctness, reason-specific rejection, projection alignment or tie-aware correctness from positional flags. Ordered results are provisional. A complete five-variant scenario requires five successful positional matches. Partial inputs are permitted; do not interpret them as complete benchmark coverage. Existing result_semantics, ordered_semantics and rejection_semantics remain the authoritative components for reviewed inputs. Stage 2 must connect explicit review evidence to those components and validate full manifest coverage before final freeze.
