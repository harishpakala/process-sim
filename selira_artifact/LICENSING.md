# RC01 licensing policy

Approved intended licensing model (2026-11-28): Apache-2.0 for original software and CC-BY-4.0 for original research data and documentation. This document records release intent, not a present grant of rights. Copyright ownership and authority to license must be confirmed before applying license texts.

| Component | Intended license | Release condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SELiRA original software, evaluation scripts, executable specifications | Apache-2.0 | Confirm copyright holders and authority, including employer or institutional rights |
| SAFSL implementation | Apache-2.0 where authorized | Verify upstream license and preserve notices |
| Original ONTO:NEXUS ontologies and TEP-Nexus datasets | CC-BY-4.0 where authorized | Verify provenance, upstream terms and redistribution rights |
| Original research documentation and experimental results | CC-BY-4.0 | Confirm rights; exclude publisher-controlled content |
| Third-party materials | Original applicable terms | Identify versions, licenses, notices and redistribution conditions |

Official texts: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode .

Before release, record rights-holder approval, attach applicable license texts and notices, verify dataset provenance and citation metadata, and update package metadata. Do not infer permission from public repository visibility. This policy does not apply to the rest of process-sim. RC01-G remains open until these conditions are met.
